## 1. Service

1.1 Telloya is an AI receptionist for the operating location named on an accepted offer for a Basic, Growth, or Pro plan. It handles calls only within the configuration an administrator has recorded for that location. The subscription includes one receptionist telephone number for that location.

1.2 The subscription includes the monthly plan named on the offer, the included minutes named on the offer, and standard setup. Optional Relaunch Guard is Google Business Profile monitoring at £9.99 per month for each location an administrator has confirmed on the offer. Interest shown on an enquiry form is not a purchase. A Guard-only location does not include a receptionist or a receptionist number.

1.3 Custom plans are available only by a separately approved written quotation that states the monthly GBP price, included minutes, and location and number quantities.

1.4 Telloya does not provide legal, medical, or emergency services. ProfileRelaunch case work and other ProfileRelaunch products are separate services unless an accepted offer expressly includes them.

1.5 Payment of an invoice does not by itself make the receptionist live. Activation follows the recorded setup sequence for the subscription. The customer enables telephone forwarding at the customer’s own telephone provider only when Telloya asks. Recording a number or forwarding instructions does not enable forwarding and does not make the service live.

## 2. Fees and included minutes

2.1 The monthly charge is the amount on the accepted offer, in pounds sterling (GBP). For a standard plan that amount is the plan price plus £9.99 for each confirmed Guard location on that offer. Before Guard, Basic is £49, Growth is £79, and Pro is £120 per month.

2.2 Basic includes 120 minutes, Growth includes 300 minutes, and Pro includes 500 minutes in each monthly billing period. Custom included minutes are only those stated on the approved quotation.

2.3 Included minutes that are not used in a monthly billing period expire at the end of that period and do not roll over.

2.4 Standard setup is included in the monthly price. Complex setup, if needed, is described in a separate written quotation and is not part of the monthly subscription price. That work starts only after the customer accepts that quotation.

2.5 How minutes are measured for a live service will be confirmed before go-live. Until a minute meter is implemented and verified, this agreement does not claim that minutes are already being counted automatically in the product.

## 3. Additional minutes (overage)

3.1 Additional minutes are charged at £0.25 per minute only when the customer has explicitly opted in on that offer and has accepted a hard spending cap for the period. An enquiry-form interest in overage does not authorise charges. The cap is the maximum additional charge authorised for the period; the cap itself is not a charge.

3.2 The product can store the opt-in and the cap. It does not yet stop further calls at the cap or raise an overage invoice automatically. Until those controls are implemented and verified, overage is not an operating promise beyond storing the customer’s choice.

3.3 Any charge for a partial minute requires a separate reviewed rounding rule before it is used. No partial-minute charge is authorised by this candidate alone.

## 4. Numbers and forwarding

4.1 Telloya may record one receptionist number and forwarding instructions for the subscribed location. The customer remains responsible for the customer’s existing telephone service and for switching forwarding on or off at that provider.

4.2 The number-owner record may reserve a number for the business. Correction or release of a reserved number follows ProfileRelaunch’s recorded process and is not automatic by this agreement alone.

## 5. AI voice and privacy

5.1 The receptionist is an AI voice. Before it asks a caller for a name or a problem, the caller is told that an AI is speaking and how the call is handled, once that disclosure is configured for the live service.

5.2 ProfileRelaunch’s commercial policy is to retain a structured lead summary and the caller details needed to act on it, and not to retain a full transcript or call audio in ProfileRelaunch systems. Caller data is not authorised for AI-model training. Lead details and summaries are retained for 90 days, subject to deletion or erasure requests and documented legal holds.

5.3 Provider storage settings, provider training terms, international transfers, and the published Telloya privacy supplement remain subject to separate confirmation. This agreement does not claim that every provider account setting already matches the policy above until that confirmation exists.

## 6. Billing, renewal, and cancellation

6.1 The accepted offer authorises a recurring monthly charge in GBP at the accepted total until the subscription ends. Changing Guard locations or plan after acceptance requires a new offer.

6.2 Either party may end the subscription with effect from the end of the period already paid. There is no automatic pro-rata refund of the current paid period. That rule is subject to applicable law and to any remedy that applies where the service has failed.

6.3 A customer self-service cancellation command is not yet enabled. Until it is, cancellation is handled through the recorded support process.

## 7. Availability and remedies

7.1 The receptionist can be unavailable because of the customer’s telephone provider, the voice provider, network conditions, or ProfileRelaunch. The service is limited to the recorded configuration and the included minutes for the period.

7.2 This candidate does not set a service-level credit schedule or a liability cap. Those terms require owner and legal confirmation before any customer is shown them.

## 8. Customer responsibilities

8.1 The person who accepts the offer confirms they have authority to bind the business named on the offer.

8.2 The customer keeps business and location details accurate and uses the receptionist for lawful business calls.

8.3 The customer agrees to the monthly GBP price shown on the offer and to forward calls only when Telloya asks.
