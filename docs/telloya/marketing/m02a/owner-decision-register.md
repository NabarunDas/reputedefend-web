## Decisions that block publication even if the commercial table is accepted

Counsel still has to review recording mode, retention, liability, governing law, controller and processor roles, and the relationship with the company privacy notice before any customer sees Telloya service terms.

An accountant still has to confirm VAT registration before any tax sentence is used. That confirmation does not set `productionTaxBehaviour`.

Provider confirmation is still required for countries, carriers, voices, recording, training, retention, deletion, metering, outages, and emergency-call behaviour.
