# Telloya privacy supplement

**Candidate for owner and legal review. Not approved. Not published. Not a website notice until separately authorised.**

**Proposed title:** Telloya privacy supplement  
**Read with:** the ProfileRelaunch company privacy notice. This supplement would address Telloya enquiry and receptionist processing that the company notice does not yet describe. It does not replace the company notice for other ProfileRelaunch products.

ProfileRelaunch Ltd (company number 17520826, registered in England and Wales, registered office 71–75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London, WC2H 9JQ) operates Telloya as a service of that company. Contact: contact@profilerelaunch.com.

---

## 1. Who this supplement covers

1.1 Business contacts who submit a Telloya enquiry or manage a Telloya subscription.

1.2 Callers whose details may be collected when a Telloya receptionist is live for a customer business.

1.3 Roles (controller or processor) for caller data collected on a business’s behalf remain subject to legal confirmation and are not decided by this candidate alone.

## 2. Business enquiries and accounts

2.1 An enquiry may include business name, country, trade, locations, website, contact name, email, phone, missed-call problem, phone setup preferences, plan interest, Guard interest, and related notes, plus a privacy-acceptance flag and a Telloya reference.

2.2 Purpose: to respond to the request, prepare a demonstration or offer when appropriate, and operate the account if the business later accepts a subscription.

2.3 Legal bases for this processing require legal confirmation before publication. Candidate bases under discussion include steps at the contact’s request before a contract and legitimate interests in answering the enquiry and limiting abuse.

## 3. Caller information

3.1 If the receptionist is switched on, a caller may be asked for a name, a callback number, the nature of the request, and a preferred callback window. The business receives a structured summary by the agreed method.

3.2 Commercial policy: ProfileRelaunch retains a structured lead summary and the caller details needed to act on it. It does not retain a full transcript or call audio in ProfileRelaunch systems. Caller content is not authorised for AI-model training.

3.3 Lead details and summaries are retained for 90 days, with deletion or erasure requests and documented legal holds as exceptions. Usage and payment evidence follow their own retention rules and are not deleted solely by that 90-day lead schedule.

3.4 Audio recording and transcription modes for the voice provider are not claimed as configured by this candidate. The published notice must match the mode that is actually verified for the account. This candidate does not say calls are recorded, or that recordings are deleted, unless that behaviour is independently confirmed.

## 4. Providers and transfers

4.1 Telloya uses shared ProfileRelaunch infrastructure, including hosting, database, email delivery, and (when enabled) a voice provider. The company notice already names certain website subprocessors. A Telloya-specific subprocessor list and any international transfer mechanism require confirmation before publication and must not invent countries or safeguards.

4.2 Provider account settings that affect storage and training must be verified for this account before live calls. Repository policy forbids authorising caller data for model training; provider contract terms are not assumed verified by this text.

## 5. Rights and contact

5.1 Individuals may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, and a copy of their data, and may complain to a supervisory authority, as described in the company privacy notice.

5.2 Routing of a caller’s request (to the customer business, to ProfileRelaunch, or both) requires legal confirmation before this supplement is published.

5.3 Contact for privacy requests: contact@profilerelaunch.com, until a different published contact is authorised.

## 6. Status of this document

6.1 This text is a review candidate only. It is not approved and must not be published on the Telloya or ProfileRelaunch websites until owner and legal authorisation.

6.2 It does not claim that every listed control already operates automatically in production.
